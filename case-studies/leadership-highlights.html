My Role Design Leadership
Focus Ops · People · Community

I am a people lead for product designers, design operation managers, and user researchers. I invest in the people and culture behind the work by coaching designers, growing managers, shaping community, and standing up for inclusion.

Coaching designers to growth

Managing a diverse set of designers — in culture, seniority, and aspiration

I've coached designers into their next promotion, and sat with others who were quietly burning out. I've reignited people who'd been in the same seat for years and needed a reason to care again, and helped others leave a role gracefully instead of letting frustration turn into attrition, and led teams through reorgs where the ground kept shifting under everyone. Along the way I've managed designers across hubs in Canada, Italy, Sweden, Germany, and China, each bringing a different relationship to hierarchy, feedback, and ambition.

I've learned no two designers are alike, and management isn't a formula. My job is finding where what a designer wants from their career and craft overlaps with what the org needs from their talent, and adjusting that balance as both sides change. Nurturing a designer's growth and seeing the impact come alive in the org is my favourite way to see my impact scale.

Design Manager Development Program

Developing design managers

Design Managers at Zalando often felt isolated: stunted growth, no real support network, nowhere to admit they were struggling. I built a curriculum and ritual around that gap, a safe space focused specifically on how to develop as a design manager, not just a designer. Sharing what actually works, comparing notes on ways of working, having a sparring partner on the hard days, treating management as a craft worth practicing deliberately. The curriculum covers bar-raising teams, management career paths, managing up, leading when nobody on your team fits the "normal" mold, motivating a checked-out report, and difficult conversations about performance.

16 design managers have been through it, and the response has been strong. Leaders can't just nurture their teams. We have to nurture our own growth too, or we run out of what we're giving away.

Design Community & Thought Leadership

Sharing the craft on stages, podcasts, and in mentorship

From conference stages across Europe to on-site masterclasses and three seasons of my own podcast, I've built a practice around one question: what does design strategy actually look like when it's working? Designers carry a unique toolkit, research instincts, pattern recognition, facilitation, conceptual thinking, and most organisations don't know how to deploy it strategically. I make the case for why they should, and show what that looks like in practice.

The themes I keep returning to: decision-making velocity, the discipline of knowing what not to build, and telling a passing fad from a business-critical pivot.
